India Rain + Lightning

This experimental map combines the public RainViewer radar composite for India with time-matched strokes from the Indian Lightning Detection Network.

Update check
Every five minutes
Observed frames
Nominally every ten minutes
Between observations
A continuous cross-fade between adjacent source frames
Lightning history
Previous-hour strokes at every timeline position, coloured and sized by age
Map resolution
Web-Mercator zoom 6, approximately 2–3 km per pixel over India
Radar cleaning
Persistent radial spokes are inpainted; clearly corrupt block frames are reconstructed from clean time neighbours

Radar and lightning can be delayed, unavailable, or incomplete. Automated quality control may miss artefacts or alter genuine echo; untouched radar frames remain in the private archive. Do not use this service for safety-critical decisions.
